## About MAGIS TBI

ADNU – M.A.G.I.S. TBI is a university-owned and operated technology business incubator serving technology-focused or technology-utilizing growth-oriented startups.

With development and scale in mind, it aims to provide access to market and resources, allowing startups to propel into success.

The word **"MAGIS"** in MAGIS TBI is an acronym for:

* **M**aximizing
* **A**lliances for
* **G**reater
* **I**nnovative
* **S**ociety

### Vision & Mission

* **Vision:** We envision MAGIS TBI as the Bicol region's launchpad for innovation—where tech-driven dreams take flight, guided by mentorship, collaboration, and Ignatian values.


* **Mission:** To build not just thriving startups, but purpose-driven leaders—men and women for others—fueling progress in their communities and beyond.


* **Tagline:** Empowering Tech for Good.



### Our Goals

* Turn bold ideas into sustainable, high-impact startups


* Empower entrepreneurs and MSMEs through hands-on programs and support


* Drive innovation and create lasting opportunities across the Bicol Region

## Facilities & Spaces

**Home of ADNU – MAGIS TBI:**

The ADNU - MAGIS TBI is currently housed at the **2nd Floor of the Matteo Ricci, S.J. Hall** - College of Business and Accountancy Entrepreneurship Facilities.

* **Co-Working Spaces:** Modern, flexible workspaces for incubatees and partner clients, open to both university-affiliated and external startups.


* **Board Room:** A professional setting for intimate meetings, strategic sessions, and investor or partner presentations.


* **TBI Roof Deck:** A vibrant open space for events, startup launches, and community gatherings—designed to inspire innovation and connection.


* **Internet-of-Things Laboratory:** Access to 3D Printing Services, IoT Lab Equipment, and hands-on technical training to bring prototypes to life.
